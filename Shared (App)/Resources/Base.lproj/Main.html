NoMoreCookies

Cookie banners, newsletter pop-ups, “open in app” nags, anti-adblock walls and floating video players — gone before you see them.

You can turn on NoMoreCookies’s Safari extension in Settings.

You can turn on NoMoreCookies’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.

NoMoreCookies’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in Safari Extensions preferences.

NoMoreCookies’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in Safari Extensions preferences.

On iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.
  2. Tap NoMoreCookies and switch it on.
  3. Tap All Websites and choose Allow.
One thing worth doing. The extension can only hide what it is allowed to see. Set website access to Allow on Every Website — otherwise Safari asks again on every new domain and banners get a head start.
